The product

ToFlip is a multi-vendor thrift fashion marketplace for mobile. Buyers discover and buy second-hand clothing from individual sellers and verified stores; sellers list items, manage inventory, track orders and chat with buyers.

I built it on my own, end to end: the Flutter app, the Supabase backend, and the role system behind customers, sellers, moderators and admins.

Three journeys

Buyers, sellers and the community each get first-class flows. None of them is bolted on to the others.

  1. Buy

    Masonry discovery feed → search & filters → product detail with condition, size and reviews → cart, address book, shipping choice and coupon → order tracking timeline and GST invoice PDF.

  2. Sell

    4-step listing wizard → inventory management (stock, edit, delete) → seller dashboard with revenue, orders, views and follower analytics → own storefront page.

  3. Connect

    Stories and social posts with likes, threaded comments and saves → chat with sellers, including product cards and price offers → push notifications.

Four roles

Trust is what makes thrift commerce work, so moderation and admin tools were built in from the start.

  • CustomerBrowse, search, wishlist, cart & checkout, orders, chat, offers, follow vendors, reviews.
  • SellerEverything a customer can do, plus listings, inventory, dashboard analytics and replying to reviews.
  • ModeratorHide or restore reviews, resolve or dismiss reports, view moderation logs.
  • Super AdminApprove, suspend and verify sellers; moderate listings; oversee orders and disputes; categories, banners and settings.
  • Trust signalsVerified-purchase, featured and pinned review badges; condition labels; ratings.
  • PolishLight & dark themes, liquid-glass bottom navigation, skeleton loaders, hero transitions.

Under the hood

A Flutter app with Riverpod state management and GoRouter navigation, backed by Supabase. Store builds and uploads are automated with Fastlane.
