The product

SPM & Lalans run scholarship assessments for students preparing for NEET, JEE and Integrated courses. I built the full-stack platform that runs these exams online, with separate student and admin portals and real-time result processing.

It has supported 5,000+ users, with backend APIs designed for high-volume, concurrent use, since many students sit the test at the same time.

How it works

Three steps for students, with admins approving each one before the test.

  1. Register

    Students fill in the registration form with their details and choose a course.

  2. Take the test

  3. Get results

    Scores are processed in real time, and students see their result and potential scholarship opportunities.

Also at SPM: a self-updating magazine shelf

I built a custom WordPress plugin that checks a Google Sheet for new monthly magazine entries. When a new issue is listed, the plugin automatically adds a download/view button to the magazine section of the website.

No one needs to edit the site by hand for each issue any more, which saves the content team hours every month.
